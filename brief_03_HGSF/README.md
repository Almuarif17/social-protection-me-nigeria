9| ## What this brief is
10| 
11| The National Home-Grown School Feeding Programme (NHGSFP) is the Federal Government of Nigeria's commitment to providing one daily mid-day meal to pupils in Primary 1–3 across public primary schools.
12| 
13| This brief is the independent M&E specification for a verification system that should already exist. It examines the programme through five analytical lenses:
14| 
15| 1. **What it committed to do** — the official five-pillar design and Results Framework architecture
16| 2. **What the evidence shows** — performance against committed targets through programme data, donor assessments, audit reports, and independent academic studies
17| 3. **Where the M&E framework falls short** — gaps in monitoring architecture and data availability
18| 4. **What the programme has genuinely achieved** — documented successes in reach, enrolment, employment, and smallholder agriculture
19| 5. **What should be added to the framework** — five operational, costed, time-bound recommendations
20| 
21| The brief draws on **34 curated public sources** spanning official government portals, World Bank and UN assessments, legislative oversight reports, ICPC fraud investigations, peer-reviewed academic studies, and media investigations.

53| ## Headline findings
54| 
55| **Finding 1 — The budget arithmetic that does not reconcile.** Component years disclosed sum to ₦219.8bn, not the stated cumulative ₦186bn.
56| 
57| **Finding 2 — The ₦70 question.** The unit cost was set at ₦70 in 2016 and held there until the 2024 suspension. Real-terms purchasing power has collapsed to ₦11.
58| 
59| **Finding 3 — Three orders of magnitude.** Documented fraud incidents are dwarfed by the ₦30 billion+ NSIPA recovery action announced December 2025.
60| 
61| **Finding 4 — The measurement blind spot.** No public NHGSFP dashboard exists. No quarterly state-level delivery record has been published since 2020.
62|

65| ## Five recommendations
66| 
67| 1. **Public quarterly dashboard** — NSIPA + FME, feasible in 90 days, no legislation required.
68| 2. **Independent verification budget line** — ≥1% of NHGSFP annual budget for accredited CSO verification, 2027 appropriation.
69| 3. **Biometric cook registry** — NIN-anchored, NSIPA + NIMC, pilot 10,000 cooks across 2 states within 180 days.
70| 4. **Automatic unit-cost review trigger** — legislated, NBS Food CPI ≥25% movement compels rate review within 60 days.
71| 5. **State co-financing readiness audit** — NSIPA + State MoEs, scores published for 12 pilot states within 6 months.
