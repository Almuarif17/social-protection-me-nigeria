# Brief Documents

The finished narrative outputs for the N-Power M&E review, in three reader-targeted formats.

---

## What is in this folder

### 📄 Full brief, 43 pages

**File:** `NPower_Independent _M&E Review.pdf`

The complete independent monitoring and evaluation review. This is the defensible reference document. It contains:

- Executive summary and methodological note.
- Eight sections, from origin and provenance through recommendations.
- Six explicit limitations, disclosed rather than concealed.
- Five annexes: the full source matrix, Theory of Change measurement coverage, glossary of acronyms, standards-compliance audit against MERL writing standards, and outstanding data actions register.

Read this if you want the full argument, the working behind every figure, and the ability to check any claim against its source.

### 📄 Executive summary, 9 pages

**File:** `NPower_Independent_M&E_Summary.pdf`

A condensed A4 executive brief that carries the same argument in less time. It contains:

- One cover page with four headline statistics.
- Two pages of findings, one card per finding.
- Three chart pages, each with the underlying data table below.
- Two pages of recommendations with all ten items and both preconditions.
- One closer with the link back to the full brief.

---

## Which document to read

| If you are | Read this |
|---|---|
| A policy officer skimming for the takeaway | Executive summary |
| A researcher checking a specific claim | Full brief, plus source matrix in Annex A |
| A journalist sourcing a figure | Executive summary first, then the full brief for the citation |
| A civil society organisation building on the analysis | Full brief, plus the reproducible analysis folder |

## Reproducing the charts

All three charts in these documents are generated by the notebook in the sister folder, [Reproducible data Analysis](../Reproducible%20data%20Analysis). Every chart's underlying CSV is public, every source is cited, and the pipeline runs end-to-end in Jupyter or Colab.
