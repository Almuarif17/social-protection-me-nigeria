# Charts

Three publication-quality PNGs used throughout the N-Power Brief 4 review. Standalone, 300 DPI, ready for reuse under CC-BY 4.0 with attribution.

---

## The three charts

### Chart 1, Federal budget transparency

**File:** `01_budget_transparency.png`

*What can and cannot be known about N-Power spending, 2016 to 2024.*

Over nine programme years, only one N-Power-specific spending figure exists in the public record (₦26.4bn, verified for May 2016). Four years (2020 to 2023) show ministry-level envelopes where N-Power's share within them is undisclosed. Four years (2017 to 2019, and 2024) have no disclosed N-Power figure at all. The empty bars are the finding.

Sources: TheCable (2019, 2021) for the 2016 verified anchor; BudgIT tracker (2020 to 2023) for the ministry envelopes.

### Chart 2, Reach verification

**File:** `02_reach_verification.png`

*Beneficiary claims against independent verification, N-Power batches.*

The horizontal bar chart shows the gold official claim against the navy independently-verified range for each batch and sub-strand. Ghost-name uncertainty (9 to 18 per cent of claimed reach) is marked as red tick brackets bounding the verified range. N-Teach and N-Health cumulative claims have no independent verification stream at all and are labelled accordingly.

Sources: N-Power Information Guide (2017); NSIPA (2026); Premium Times (2019); Punch (2020); TheCable (2019, 2021); FIJ (2024); ANEEJ (2025); Osimen et al. (2025); Guardian Nigeria (2024).

### Chart 3, Stipend erosion

**File:** `03_stipend_erosion.png`

*Real-terms value of the ₦30,000 N-Power monthly stipend, NBS Food CPI deflated, 2016 to 2026.*

The nominal stipend line sits flat at ₦30,000. The deflated real-value line falls from ₦30,000 in 2016 to a ₦3,000 to ₦5,000 band by 2026. Annotations mark the launch point, the endpoint band, and the silent-erosion note. The bottom callout gives the implied 2026 restoration stipend (₦180,000 to ₦300,000), the rate needed to match 2016 real value.

Source: NBS Consumer Price Index, Food sub-index (annual averages). 2026 band reflects re-basing sensitivity, since NBS revised its CPI base year to 2024 = 100.

## Technical details

- **Format:** PNG, 300 DPI, matplotlib-generated on white background.
- **Palette:** navy `#0a2342` and gold `#c9a227`, with red `#a83232` for uncertainty and critical annotations.
- **Aspect:** landscape, sized for print in an A4 document at full page width.

---

## How the charts are generated

All three charts are rendered by the Jupyter notebook in the sister folder, [Reproducible data Analysis](../Reproducible%20data%20Analysis). Every underlying value sits in a CSV in that folder. Clone the folder, install `pandas` and `matplotlib`, run the notebook, and the three PNGs are regenerated byte-identical.
