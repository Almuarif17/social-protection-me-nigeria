# Brief Reading Documents

The finished narrative outputs for Brief 5 in two reader-targeted formats.

---

## What is in this folder

### 📄 Full brief, 52 pages

**File:** `Brief_05_The Jourey_of_CCT.pdf`

The complete independent monitoring and evaluation review. This is the defensible reference document. It contains:

- Executive summary and methodological note at the front.
- Twelve sections tracing the CCT paradigm from its Southern origins through global diffusion into Nigeria's COPE and NASSP-HUP.
- Four publication-quality charts inline: transfer adequacy across programmes, coverage as share of population, Nigeria's real-value trajectory 2016 to 2026, and the CCT effect hierarchy by outcome domain.
- Seven tables including recommended architecture and unimplemented commitments watch list.
- Four annexes: the full 26-source evidence matrix (six foundational, eleven country stories, three Nigeria, two cross-cutting, five supporting), programme classification by type and outcome, glossary of acronyms and terms, and follow-up reporting register.
- Full reference list with every source cited.

Read this if you want the full argument, the working behind every figure, and the ability to check any claim against its source.

### 📄 Executive summary, 17 pages

**File:** `Brief5_Executive_Summary.pdf`

A condensed A4 executive brief that carries the same argument in less time. It contains:

- One cover page with four headline statistics (1997 origin, 6-7% transfer share, 53% targeting accuracy, 3 of 4 conditions failed).
- The target question and method on page 2.
- The origin story of CCT: three Southern experiments before World Bank engagement.
- The Mexican and Brazilian consolidations.
- A single-view diffusion table covering all sixteen comparator programmes.
- Full Nigeria section (COPE, NASSP-HUP, the January 2024 suspension, the sibling N-Power finding).
- All four charts embedded with captions.
- The four-condition verdict panel.
- Seven recommendation pills with current position, required position, and evidence basis for each.
- Unimplemented commitments watch list.
- Where-to-go-from-here closer with the citation block.

## Design and format

Both documents use the same house style established in Briefs 3 and 4 of the series: navy institutional cover, gold accents, cream body pages, coloured pill numbering for recommendations, and justified body text.

Both PDFs are fully text-searchable, work on mobile and desktop readers, and are formatted for A4 print at 300 DPI.
